Methodology, date conventions and remaining coverage gaps

What this review covers

Selected major Commonwealth changes across the six requested categories. FBT, dividend imputation, personal offsets and levies are included as adjacent income-tax measures. Multiple milestones can describe one reform, so an event count is not a count of distinct tax increases.

Six 1960 records establish the starting regimes. They do not imply that those taxes began in 1960. A further 1994 income-tax benchmark is also context rather than a verified amendment event.

Dates and legal status

The event date identifies the labelled milestone. Announcement, assent, application and end dates appear separately where verified. Retrospective application can precede assent. Parliamentary passage is not Royal Assent.

A year-only marker is positioned at 1 July and a month-only marker at the 15th for layout only. These plotting positions are not claimed historical dates. Future applications are shaded; pending proposals remain hollow markers.

“Historical law event” does not mean the old provision is still operative. “Proposal” means no enactment was verified at this review date; it does not predict passage. Court judgments and ATO rulings are distinguished from legislation.

What is not yet complete

Early coverage, particularly 1960–82, is sparse. This is not an exhaustive list of annual rate adjustments, rebates, trust amendments, GST base rulings, excise tariff items or state taxes. Every routine indexation round is not separately entered. Later intra-April 2026 fuel adjustments are not fully reconstructed.

Nine selected basic resident income-tax schedules are supplied. Do not interpolate missing years or use them as a total-liability calculator: deductions, offsets, Medicare and temporary levies are excluded. Dollar thresholds are nominal, not inflation-adjusted.

Evidence and verification

Sources include enacted legislation, official histories and guidance, court records and original historical research. Some ATO material was available only through indexed excerpts; each source discloses its access basis. The data are a curated transcription, not a raw-source archive or complete primary-law audit.

Schema, references, dates and basic-rate arithmetic are checked by the accompanying compiler. Those checks do not prove completeness or resolve every legal exception. This artifact is for research review, not personal tax advice.
