Definitions, coverage and review limitations

Seats elected and chamber composition are different measures. House results include deferred division polls. “Senate elected” counts only the places filled in that contest. Older full-Senate tables describe composition after elected senators take their places; modern full-Senate figures are result projections using election-date affiliations. Neither is a live membership series.

Historical edge cases are retained. The 1908 SA supplementary Senate poll, four Senate-only elections, the 1963/1966/1969/1972 Senate vacancy contests, three supplementary House polls, four unopposed House returns, the annulled 2013 WA result and its 2014 replacement, and the later-annulled 1992 Wills return are identified.

Party counts follow source-era definitions. The early Commonwealth Liberal Party is separate from the modern Liberal Party; historical and contemporary United Australia parties are separate. Some older affiliates are grouped within Liberal/Country totals. Fourteen House result rows retain an unresolved “Other” bucket. Independents are a category, not a party. LNP is disaggregated in modern AEC House results; the 2010 Senate source instead uses Liberal/National caucus groupings.

By-election deltas are not a continuous balance-of-power ledger. They compare the winner with the outgoing member’s affiliation before the vacancy, not with the empty seat on polling day. Coalition-family and government-bloc deltas from 1949 are arithmetic conveniences, not findings about confidence, the Speaker, current coalition agreements or crossbench support. Defections, ordinary casual appointments, court recounts and every intervening vacancy are not exhaustively modelled.

Leadership dates mean substantive appointments. PM names at the poll and after government formation are shown separately. DPM coverage starts with the formal office in 1968; pre-1968 de facto deputies and routine acting arrangements are not included. Day-level leadership chronology uses an explicitly identified secondary compilation, supplemented by museum/archives and current ministry checks; it is not fully primary-audited.

This is a review edition. Three historical House by-election affiliation discrepancies are flagged. The 1963 and 1969 full-Senate counts are reconstructions requiring review; the 1966 snapshot uses a secondary table. Full-Senate snapshots for the 1908 and 1972 contests are not independently verified. Arithmetic and coverage validation do not eliminate historical classification uncertainty.
